# Research notes (September 2026)

The rules in this skill, and where they come from.

## Hooks and structure

- The first three seconds decide most of whether people keep watching; aim for a
  hook rate of 25–35%, and rework the opening if it's under 20%. Lead with motion,
  surprise or a bold claim, never a logo or slow build.
  [MB Adv](https://www.mbadv.agency/tiktok-ads/creative-best-practices),
  [Sovran](https://sovran.ai/blog/tiktok-creative-best-practices),
  [PxlPeak](https://pxlpeak.com/docs/tiktok-ads/creative)
- TikTok's guidance: state the proposition in the first 3 seconds, the hook in the
  first 6, use text overlays for context, and brand early (most top ads do).
  [Megadigital](https://megadigital.ai/en/blog/tiktok-ad-creatives/),
  [Creatify](https://creatify.ai/blog/tiktok-ads-complete-guide-to-creating-high-performing-creatives-in-2026)
- Native, creator-style content beats glossy ads.
  [Fabfunnel](https://fabfunnel.com/tiktok-ad-creative/)

## Game ads specifically

- "Fail" and "I can do better" hooks are among the top-performing mobile game
  creatives, but fake gameplay backfires: over a third of players say the ad must
  match the game for them to stay.
  [Udonis](https://www.blog.udonis.co/mobile-marketing/mobile-games/fake-mobile-game-ads),
  [MAF](https://medium.com/myappfree/the-secret-behind-fail-ads-for-mobile-games-21c765ba64ea),
  [Scrolling Media](https://www.scrolling.media/post/why-do-fake-mobile-game-ads-outperform-real-ones)
- For indie games, raw gameplay with a strong visual hook in the first 2 seconds
  beats talking heads. Organic reach has dropped since 2025, so the proven path is
  posting consistently, then boosting the winners with Spark Ads.
  [presskit.gg](https://presskit.gg/field-guides/tiktok-indie-game-marketing),
  [Gamosy](https://gamosy.com/blog/tiktok-game-marketing)

## Length, loops and frequency

- 21–34s is the sweet spot for completion; 15–30s gets the most shares and
  rewatches; loops favor 7–15s.
  [OpusClip](https://www.opus.pro/blog/tiktok-length-format-retention-data),
  [Retensis](https://retensis.com/blog/tiktok-retention-rate-benchmarks-2026)
- Brand accounts: 4–7 posts a week works if quality holds.
  [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/how-often-should-you-post-on-tiktok/)
- Shorts stops pushing videos people swipe away from, and pushes ones watched past
  80%; it rewards watch time over swipes in 2026.
  [PostEverywhere](https://posteverywhere.ai/blog/youtube-shorts-vs-tiktok)

## Visual rules

- Safe zone (1080×1920): keep text away from the top ~130 px, bottom ~480 px,
  right ~140 px.
  [Zeely](https://zeely.ai/blog/tiktok-safe-zones/),
  [Kreatli](https://kreatli.com/guides/tiktok-safe-zone)
- Text: bold sans-serif, strong contrast (stroke or plate), five to seven words a
  line; word-by-word captions lift watch time.
  [RocketShip HQ](https://www.rocketshiphq.com/text-overlays-video-ads-mobile/),
  [OpusClip](https://www.opus.pro/blog/youtube-shorts-caption-subtitle-best-practices)
- "Oddly satisfying" and hypnotic loops are a huge category (#oddlysatisfying has
  tens of billions of views); predictability and seamless loops keep people
  watching. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oddly_satisfying_videos),
  [Irish Times](https://www.irishtimes.com/culture/art-and-design/visual-asmr-the-weird-online-world-of-hypnotic-loops-and-satisfying-sounds-1.4525192)

## Rules we must follow

- AI label: required for realistic synthetic people, scenes and cloned voices;
  generic TTS narration isn't cloning, and brand accounts are covered.
  [TikTok newsroom](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/new-labels-for-disclosing-ai-generated-content),
  [Influencer Marketing Hub](https://influencermarketinghub.com/ai-disclosure-rules/)
- Music: business accounts may use only the Commercial Music Library, or
  licensed/own music. [TikTok](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/how-to-use-the-commercial-music-library),
  [Soundstripe](https://www.soundstripe.com/blogs/tiktok-music-library-explained)

## The audience

- chess.com's audience is mostly 25–34; 18–24s watch the most streams and prefer
  personalities and influencer events over pro events; interest overlaps with video
  games. [chess.com segments](https://www.chess.com/blog/aranhawaii/chess-consumer-segments-defined),
  [Similarweb](https://www.similarweb.com/website/chess.com/)
- Chess meme culture: "Google en passant / holy hell" (r/AnarchyChess), "THE ROOK"
  (GothamChess), "brilliant move" edits.
  [Know Your Meme](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/en-passant-google-en-passant),
  [chess.com](https://www.chess.com/blog/Sentopro2/gothamchess-is-famous-for-sacrificing-the-rooooook)
- Streamer slang: cooked, let him cook, aura, lock in, chat.
  [Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/articles/chat-cooked-guide-internet-slang-090000553.html),
  [StreamSpell](https://www.streamspell.com/post/streaming-glossary-2025)
- RTS terms: rush, turtle, boom, macro/micro, APM.
  [Wikipedia: Rush](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rush_(video_games)),
  [PCGamingWiki](https://www.pcgamingwiki.com/wiki/Glossary:RTS)
- History: chess began as chaturanga in northern India (the four limbs of the
  army); "shah mat" means the king is helpless. The chariot became the rook and the
  elephant became the bishop; the rook is still called *haathi* in India.
  [World History Encyclopedia](https://www.worldhistory.org/article/2793/the-history-of-chess/),
  [The Met](https://www.metmuseum.org/exhibitions/listings/2011/the-game-of-kings-medieval-ivory-chessmen-from-the-isle-of-lewis/exhibition-blog/game-of-kings/blog/shah-mat)
