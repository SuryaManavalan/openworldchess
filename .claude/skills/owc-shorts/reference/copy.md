# Copy: hooks, titles, mantras, CTAs

Short, punchy, emotional. People scroll fast; you have about two seconds.
**3–7 words per card. One idea per card. Big.**

## CTAs (one per video, at the end)

- Play free in your browser → openworldchess.com
- No download. Just move.
- Your first king is waiting.
- Link in bio. Claim your land.
- Comment your rating. We'll see you on the board.
- Which faction do you fight first? (comment bait for faction videos)

Always include "free" and "browser" (or "no download") somewhere, in the video
or the caption. **Every video shows openworldchess.com** (the end card at least),
and every caption ends by pointing to it ("Play free: link in bio"). The
same link is in the TikTok bio.

## Captions and hashtags

- Caption: one line that adds something the video doesn't (a question, a stat, a
  dare), then 3–5 hashtags.
- Core: `#chess #chesstok #strategygame #indiegame #openworldchess`
- Rotate one or two by topic: `#enpassant`, `#chessmemes`, `#rts`, `#ageofempires`,
  `#dnd`, `#history`, `#oddlysatisfying`, `#gamedev`, `#browsergame`.

## Voice lines (ElevenLabs)

- Match or echo the on-screen text; never contradict it.
- At most 2.5 words per second. Pauses are allowed; silence is dramatic.
- Two voices, used consistently:
  - **The Chronicler**: low, calm, mythic. For lore, mantras, big moments.
  - **The Caster**: energetic, esports/chess-streamer energy. For battles and fails.
- Don't imitate any real person's voice.

## Humor

The subtle insider layer (one reference per ad at most, never explained) lives in `humor.md`. It is dated: check its freshness before using a line from it.

## Words to avoid

"Revolutionary", "the best game ever", "download now" (there's nothing to
download), "millions of players", "pay to win", anything we can't show.
Avoid negative framing of chess ("chess is boring"); we love chess and we are
making more of it.
