# Subtle humor: the insider layer

**Last researched: 2026-09-29.** Fresh slang lasts about 4–8 weeks. Before posting any line
from the "fresh" list, check that it's still alive (Know Your Meme's weekly roundup, TikTok
search), and redo this research if the date above is more than a month old.

The idea: every line reads as a straight, stoic line to a stranger. The people who know
catch the reference, and nobody gets it explained to them. The humor is for insiders (a
little gatekeep-y), never a wink at the whole audience.

## Rules

1. **One reference per ad at most**, and only in about one ad out of every two or three. The line must work without it.
2. **Where it goes:** best as the on-screen text at a beat or on the end card. Next best: voice, said deadpan. A second, deeper cut goes only in the caption or a pinned comment, never in the hook's first second.
3. **Never explain it.** No hashtag for the meme, no winking emoji, no "iykyk". (The one exception is 👌 after "Fairs", because it *is* the reference.)
4. **Borrow words only.** Never the meme's clip, audio, image or face: that dates the ad instantly and brings likeness and licensing risk.
5. **Stay in the brand voice.** Short, declarative, stoic; the joke is an understatement. If it needs an exclamation mark, cut it.
6. **Punch at the board, not at people.** No real names or faces, no controversies, no politics, no jokes about looks or weight, and no slang with misogynist or manosphere roots.
7. **Watch the shelf life.** Don't schedule a fresh reference more than about 3 weeks out. Evergreen chess canon is safe anytime.

## Evergreen chess canon (safe anytime; don't overuse)

The king walking first (bongcloud) · "Oh no. My queen." said deadpan while the sacrifice
works (Eric Rosen) · "Guess the Elo." · "He played f6." (GothamChess) · "Take. Take. Take."
used only as rhythm, never as the app's name · Chess.com review labels ("Brilliant", "a
miss") as plain words, never their icons · "Two bishops. One understanding." (Il Vaticano,
a deep cut) · "X diff" in on-screen text ("Elephant diff.") · "GG." · El Clásico (Hikaru
vs Magnus: the idea of a rivalry, no names).

## Dead or off-limits

- **Dead:** 6-7 and the number memes; skibidi, sigma, gyatt, Ohio, fanum tax, mewing and rizz; "brainrot" and Italian brainrot; sussy, poggers, ratio, "chat is this real"; "it's giving", slay, demure, delulu; the Google en passant / holy hell / new response just dropped chain (showing en passant is fine; quoting the chain isn't); "the rook"; Mittens; "knightmare".
- **Off-limits:**
  - brick (crude origin);
  - CaseOh jokes;
  - "mog" (looksmaxxing roots);
  - "clanker" (it works as a mock slur, and it knocks our own AI camps);
  - "you play games, we don't play games" (the source clip is a racist tirade);
  - "discombobulator";
  - huzz, bop, foid, chud;
  - any real person's controversy: Naroditsky (respectful tribute only), Kramnik, Niemann, gambling streams.
- **Plain English now, so no joke value:** cooked, crash out, lowkey, W/L (fine in comment replies only), NPC (only literally, for wild camps), based (political baggage; avoid).

## Worked examples (in our voice)

- Troops out: "Quiet on the creek. The army moves at dawn."
- Hook: "No banners. No drums. Quiet on the creek."
- En passant: "He took it in passing. …Fairs."
- Calling troops home: "Some kings chase glory. This one chose la peace."
- Promotion: "Crowned for one war. Washed by winter."
- King founds a hamlet: "The king walks first. He has land to hold."
- Hook on a camp fight: "Guess the Elo. Then guess again."
- Reinforcements: "It was so over. Then the knight arrived."
- A bait sacrifice: "Oh no. My queen."
- A blunder: "He played f6. We don't speak of him."
- A trade sequence: "Take. Take. Take. Then the elephant."
- Checkmate: "Aura isn't posed for. It's won on the board."
