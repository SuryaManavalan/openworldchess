# Who we're talking to, and how they talk

## The viewer

**Someone who plays chess *and* plays video games.** Usually 16–34, mostly male
but not only, very online. They:

- play rapid and blitz on their phone, know their rating to the point, and
  have opinions about openings;
- watched the chess boom happen (streamers, PogChamps, *The Queen's Gambit*,
  memes), and love chess memes as much as chess;
- grew up on strategy games: Age of Empires, StarCraft, Clash of Clans, Civ,
  Total War, Minecraft, Stellaris, with a soft spot for "one more turn";
- watch Twitch and YouTube; speak in chat;
- like history and myth: empires, sieges, dynasties, dragons, D&D.

## How they think (what makes them stop)

| Their instinct | What to show them |
|---|---|
| "I'm good at chess, but chess is always the same 64 squares." | The board never ends. Chess, but it's a whole world. |
| "My rating is who I am." | The land has a rating. Richer land, stronger neighbors. Climb it. |
| "I've always wondered what happens *after* checkmate." | After the mate, you take the town. Their pieces flee; their buildings are yours. |
| "Strategy games are all build orders and clicking." | Your build order ends in a real chess game. APM doesn't save you; calculation does. |
| "I'd never lose to *that*." (the "I can do better" itch) | A blunder, a hanging queen, a camp that looks weak but isn't. Let them feel smarter than the video. |
| "Is this real or another fake mobile ad?" | Real footage, real site in the frame, the URL, "free in your browser". Earn trust fast. |
| "Chess memes." | En passant is in. Promotion is temporary. The rook is an elephant. We're in on the jokes. |
| Awe, collection, lore | 32 factions, a Dragon Brood, relics, wonders, titles from Settler to High King. |

**Emotions that work for this crowd:** mastery ("I'd see that"), outrage-lite
("he did NOT just hang the queen"), curiosity ("wait, what happens next?"), awe
(the zoom-out), status (titles, crowns, ratings), calm (the hypnotic pieces).

## Lingo

Use it like a native, sparingly: one or two terms per video, in the right
place. Wrong or forced slang is worse than none. If you aren't sure what a term
means, don't use it.

## Tone rules for lingo

- **Chess terms** can be literal; they're our home turf.
- **Twitch terms** are for hooks and end cards, not the whole script.
- **RTS terms** land best as contrasts: "Macro in the world. Micro on the board."
- **History terms** give weight; use them for mantra pieces and big moments.
- Never mock the viewer. Never punch at a real person or community.
- Keep it clean: no slurs, no sexual jokes, nothing that ages the brand badly.
